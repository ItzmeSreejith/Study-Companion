Real, deterministic constraint-aware scheduling algorithm designed for education fairness

Deterministic Optimization Engine Principles

1. Hard Constraints

Never schedules before earliestAllowedDate or after latestAllowedDate. Internal exams remain 100% fixed unless marked reschedulable.

2. Workload Conservation

Total academic effort is strictly conserved (∑ Hours Before == ∑ Hours After). We redistribute, never erase.

3. Peak & Overload Minimization

Applies quadratic penalty to workloads exceeding daily threshold (τ = 4.0h) to actively squash dangerous workload spikes.

4. Inertia & Determinism

Penalizes unnecessary date shifts and respects high-priority tasks. Deterministic tie-breaking ensures 100% reproducible proposals.

Formal Algorithm Architecture

1. Objective Function

For each day $t \in [D_{start}, D_{end}]$, the daily workload is $W(t) = \sum_{a \in A, deadline(a) = t} effort(a)$. We minimize:

Cost = α × max(W(t)) + β × ∑ max(0, W(t) - τ)² + γ × ∑ (Dist(orig, new))² × PriorityWeight

2. Invariable Invariants & Hard Constraints

  • Boundary Guarantee: $\forall a \in A, \quad earliestAllowedDate(a) \le currentDeadline(a) \le latestAllowedDate(a)$.
  • Exam Immutability: If $type(a) == 'internal\_exam'$ and $reschedulable == false$, $currentDeadline(a) \equiv originalDeadline(a)$.
  • Conservation of Workload: $\sum_{a \in A} effort(a)_{before} = \sum_{a \in A} effort(a)_{after}$. No work is lost or fabricated.
  • Determinism: Ties are broken by evaluating the smallest date difference $|d - originalDeadline|$, followed by lexicographical ID order.
